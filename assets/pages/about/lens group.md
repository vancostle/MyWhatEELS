## LENS Group Overview
LENS stands for Laboratory of Electron Nanoscopies. Based on their official site, LENS is part of the MIND group (Micro-nanotechnologies and Nanoscopies for Electrophotonic Devices) within the Department of Electronics and Biomedical Engineering at the Faculty of Physics, University of Barcelona.

MIND is also a member of IN2UB (Institute of Nanoscience and Nanotechnology of the University of Barcelona), and within this context WhatEELS can be presented as part of the broader LENS research ecosystem: a practical software tool that supports data exploration and analysis workflows aligned with electron microscopy and EELS characterization needs.

---

## What They Focus On
LENS specializes in nanoscale structural and chemical characterization of materials using Transmission Electron Microscopy (TEM) techniques.

Their work emphasizes:

- characterization of nanostructured materials,
- correlation between fundamental material properties and device performance,
- advanced electron microscopy methods and training,
- collaboration with other teams and institutions.

---

## Research and Academic Environment
According to the website, much of their experimental work is carried out using the Science and Technical Services facilities of the University of Barcelona, where TEM instrumentation is located.

The group also highlights:

- active research dissemination (news, publications, projects),
- teaching and training activities,
- outreach for schools and the public,
- openness to new collaborations.

## How This Connects to WhatEELS
This alignment includes interactive analysis tasks such as spectral exploration, clustering, fitting, and quantification, which are consistent with LENS interests in nanoscale materials characterization.
