## University of Barcelona (UB)
The University of Barcelona (UB) is the institutional academic context around the LENS group and, by extension, around the WhatEELS project. In the WhatEELS documentation, this helps frame the tool not only as a standalone application, but also as part of a broader university research ecosystem.

---

## UB as a Research-Intensive University
According to the official UB research pages, UB describes itself as a research-intensive university with international impact across major branches of knowledge. The institution highlights a large research community, cross-disciplinary activity, and a strong focus on transferring research outcomes to society.

UB also emphasizes:

- broad faculty and research-group coverage across multiple fields,
- support for innovation and scientific infrastructure,
- talent attraction and international collaboration,
- participation in major research networks (including LERU).

---

## Research Environment Relevant to WhatEELS
For projects like WhatEELS, the UB context is especially important because it combines:

- advanced scientific and technical services,
- active microscopy- and materials-related research communities,
- open science and science communication initiatives,
- collaboration between research groups, institutes, and associated centers.

This environment aligns well with the goals of WhatEELS as an interactive platform for EELS data exploration, clustering, fitting, and quantification.

---

## Relationship Between UB, LENS, and WhatEELS
A practical way to present the relationship is:

- UB provides the institutional and research framework.
- LENS operates within that framework (through MIND and related UB structures).
- WhatEELS supports the analytical workflows needed in that research setting.

In other words, WhatEELS can be positioned as a research-support tool connected to the University of Barcelona ecosystem through the LENS group.
